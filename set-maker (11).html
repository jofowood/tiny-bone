DYO - Tiny Bone Set

With this tool you can design a Tiny Bone Set of your own. Each of the 17 Tiny Bone images in THE TRAY can be used only once in any arrangement on a 2×17 board, THE SET. Your Set can be any width up to 17 and 1 or 2 rows tall. Whatever outline you build has to end up a solid rectangle, four square corners, no notches or holes. You can use the solid colors to cover any gap inside your set that doesn't include a Tiny Bone.

Each Tiny Bone Set is made to order. Each Tiny Bone image is an individual original tin type. With this in mind, the images on this webpage are meant as a design tool only. Your Tiny Bone Set will be unique. If your set includes color, those spaces will be hand painted acrylic on the backing.

The backing of most Tiny Bone Sets will be wood. However, the final backing material used, its finish and it's quality is at my discretion.

Each Tiny Bone Set is unique and handmade. Pricing is quoted once your set is requested. For your reference, I'm currently estimating $45-$55/grid space. Production timelines vary.
